As a research assistant at Tilburg University, designed and ran a personalized language-learning study for Turkish–Dutch bilingual children using a **NAO social robot** paired with a tablet-based interaction, as part of the EU **Horizon 2020 L2TOR** project.

- Designed the child-facing interaction, combining robot behavior with a tablet interface for a young bilingual audience.
- Worked jointly with the Tilburg and Utrecht teams to localize the system for Turkish–Dutch bilingual children and integrate child-development and interaction requirements into the learning task.
- This project is where my interest in human–robot interaction started, and it shaped the experimental methods I later used in my PhD research.

**Tools/methods:** NAO robot (Choregraphe), tablet-based interaction design, study design for child participants
